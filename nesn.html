01

Executive Summary

Volume is coming back. Nestlé grew organically by 3.6% in H1 2026 (RIG 1.5%, pricing 2.1%), with real internal growth stepping up from 1.2% in Q1 to 1.8% in Q2. Reported sales nonetheless fell 2.5% to CHF 43.1B as a strong franc cost 6.2 points. Full-year guidance of 3–4% organic growth was confirmed.

01

Better quality of growth. RIG of 1.5% versus 0.2% a year ago; Q2 organic growth of 3.7%. Emerging markets ex-China grew 7.1% (RIG 3.9%), developed markets 2.3%.

02

Margin recovering, profit not yet. UTOP margin of 16.4%, up from 15.7% in H2 2025 but 10 bps below H1 2025. Underlying EPS CHF 2.22 (−2.4%); net profit −31.4% on restructuring and impairments.

03

Portfolio reshaping. Waters and premium beverages go into Peranel, a 50:50 JV with Platinum Equity valued at $5.6B EV, closing expected by H1 2027.

04

Cash generation intact. Free cash flow of CHF 3.4B (H1 2025: 2.3B); FY2026 FCF guided above CHF 9B. Net debt CHF 56.3B after the CHF 8.0B dividend.

02

Results Analysis

Organic Growth and Its Mix

Nestlé reported sales of CHF 43.1B in H1 2026, down 2.5% in Swiss francs but up 3.6% organically. Q2 organic growth reached 3.7%, the strongest quarter of the half. The headline is close to last year's; what changed is the mix.

Pricing has normalised from 2.7% in H1 2025 to 2.1%, while volumes (RIG) rose from 0.2% to 1.5%. Growth that rests on volume rather than price increases is more durable, and this is the clearest sign yet that the turnaround under CEO Philipp Navratil is taking hold.

Zones and Categories

Zone Asia, Oceania and Africa led with +4.3%, Zone Americas grew 3.3% (Latin America strong, notably Mexico and Brazil) and Zone Europe 2.7%. Greater China remains the weak spot at −10.6%. By category, coffee was the standout at +7.5% (RIG 2.9%), followed by waters at +5.1%; nutrition turned positive in Q2 as infant formula declines eased.

03

2026 Outlook

FY2026 Guidance

Management confirmed its full-year outlook and tightened the organic growth range to 3% to 4%, with RIG expected to keep accelerating. The UTOP margin should improve versus 2025, and free cash flow should exceed CHF 9B.

The next checkpoint is the nine-month sales release in October. We will look for RIG of at least 2% in Q3, a stabilising Greater China, and no further deterioration in the franc.

H1 free cash flow rose to CHF 3.4B on lower capex and a smaller working-capital outflow. Hitting the CHF 9B full-year target implies roughly CHF 5.6B in H2, consistent with the usual second-half weighting.

05

Conclusion

The operating turnaround is real; the earnings recovery is not there yet.

H1 2026 shows Nestlé growing on volume again, led by coffee and emerging markets, with a margin rebuilding from its H2 2025 low. The Peranel joint venture sharpens the focus on core categories. Against that, currency and restructuring keep reported profit under pressure.

We initiate at Hold with a price target of CHF 82, implying upside of +6.5%. We would turn more positive on evidence that RIG holds above 2% and the UTOP margin moves back toward 17%. The nine-month sales release in October is the next catalyst.
