01

Executive Summary

Growth is still accelerating. NVIDIA reported Q2 FY2027 revenue of $96.2B, up 106% year-on-year and 18% sequentially, against consensus of $92.2B. Year-on-year growth has now accelerated for five straight quarters. Q3 is guided to $108B ±2%, with no China data center compute revenue assumed.

01

Clear beat. Revenue $96.2B (+4.4% vs. consensus); non-GAAP EPS of $2.22 (GAAP $2.46, boosted by investment gains) against roughly $2.06 expected.

02

Data Center is 93% of revenue. $89.0B (+117% year-on-year). Hyperscalers contributed $49B (+13% sequentially); neoclouds, industrial and enterprise customers the remaining ~$40B.

03

Margins holding at the top. Gross margin of 75.0% for a third quarter running; Q3 guided at 74.0% ±50 bps as Vera Rubin ramps.

04

Long runway. Management targets roughly 70% revenue growth in FY2028, with hyperscale demand expected to reaccelerate from Q4 as Vera Rubin supply grows.

02

Results Analysis

Growth Trajectory

Revenue reached $96.2B, beating consensus by $4.05B. More striking is the direction: year-on-year growth went from 56% a year ago to 106% this quarter, on a base that is itself twice as large. At the midpoint, Q3 guidance implies about 89% growth.

Data Center and the new Edge Computing segment (Gaming, Professional Visualization, Automotive and OEM, reported together since FY2027) both grew sequentially. Networking had another record quarter, up 18% sequentially, with Spectrum-X Ethernet up 2.6x year-on-year.

Data Center Build-out

Data Center revenue has more than doubled in a year, from $41.1B to $89.0B. Hyperscalers remain the largest buyers at $49B, but the rest of the market (neoclouds, sovereign AI, industrial and enterprise) is now about $40B a quarter, which reduces reliance on a handful of customers.

03

Outlook

Q3 FY2027 Guidance

NVIDIA guided Q3 revenue to $108B ±2% ($105.8B–$110.2B), about 12% sequential growth, with gross margin of 74.0% ±50 bps. The outlook assumes no Data Center compute sales to China, so any resumption of exports would be upside.

05

Conclusion

Bigger than ever, and still speeding up.

NVIDIA continues to defy the law of large numbers: revenue doubled year-on-year to almost $100B a quarter, margins held at 75%, and guidance points to further growth without China. The broadening customer base and the Rubin ramp support a multi-year outlook.

We reiterate Buy with a price target of $280, implying upside of +22.6%. The next catalysts are Q3 FY2027 results in late November and early evidence on Vera Rubin volumes.
