01

Executive Summary

One weak line, three strong ones. Swissquote's H1 2026 net revenues rose 1.7% to CHF 364.2M and pre-tax profit eased 1.2% to CHF 182.9M, a 50.2% margin. The miss was entirely crypto, down 66% to CHF 14.6M. Fees, interest and trading all grew, and client assets hit a record CHF 96.3B.

01

Crypto was the whole story. Crypto income fell from CHF 43.1M to CHF 14.6M. Excluding it, revenues grew by roughly 10%.

02

Growth engine intact. Net new monies of CHF 5.1B, the second-best half-year ever; client assets +19.8% year-on-year; 64,000 new Swissquote and 24,000 new Yuh accounts.

03

Guidance cut. FY2026 net revenues lowered to ~CHF 730M (from 760M) and pre-tax profit to ~CHF 365M (from 385M), with the margin still above 50%.

04

Market over-reacted. Shares fell 12.9% on results and now trade at CHF 36.16, near the 52-week low, about 18x our FY2026 EPS estimate.

02

Results Analysis

Flat Headline, Solid Core

Net revenues of CHF 364.2M were broadly flat on both H1 and H2 2025. Pre-tax profit of CHF 182.9M (−1.2%) and net profit of CHF 153.6M (−2.9%) reflect higher investment in growth on top of the crypto shortfall. Diluted EPS was CHF 1.02 versus CHF 1.05 a year ago (post-split).

Profit held up despite the revenue shortfall: the pre-tax margin remained above 50%, in line with management's long-standing target.

Revenue Lines

Fee and commission income excluding crypto grew +13% to CHF 123.7M on higher trading volumes and assets. Net interest income rose 7.2% to CHF 115.9M despite low Swiss rates, helped by growing client cash. Trading income, including eForex, rose 5% to CHF 107.5M. Crypto now makes up just 4% of revenues.

03

2026 Outlook

Revised FY2026 Guidance

Management now guides to net revenues of about CHF 730M and pre-tax profit of about CHF 365M, assuming only a gradual crypto recovery late in the year. That implies an H2 roughly equal to H1, with little crypto contribution: a conservative base.

Client assets are the best leading indicator: at CHF 96.3B they are within reach of CHF 100B, and every franc of assets generates custody, interest and trading revenue. Yuh, now with 423,000 clients and CHF 4B of assets, is targeting 500,000 clients.

The group now has more than 1.2 million accounts. Account growth matters more than crypto volumes for the long-term value of the platform.

05

Conclusion

A cyclical miss on a structural growth story.

H1 2026 was disappointing on the surface, but the shortfall came from the smallest and most volatile revenue line. Assets, accounts and the three core revenue lines all grew, and the margin stayed above 50%.

We rate Swissquote Overweight with a price target of CHF 44, implying upside of +21.7%. Catalysts are client assets crossing CHF 100B, any recovery in crypto volumes, and the FY2026 results in early 2027.
